Privacy Policy

CatSplit | Last updated: October 2, 2026 | This is an English translation of the Chinese version. If the two differ, the Chinese version prevails.

CatSplit (the "Service") is a free expense-splitting tool run personally by Quinn Yen ("I" or "me"). This policy explains what data the Service collects, how it is used, and what you can do with your own data.

1. Data we collect

  • LINE account data: signing in with LINE gives us your LINE user ID, display name and avatar URL. We do not get your phone number, email address or friend list.
  • Guest names: you can join without signing in to LINE by entering your own name from a group link (group members can also add a name on your behalf). We only record the name you enter and generate an identifier that stands for that name. We get no other personal data about you. The names you picked (up to 3 groups) are stored only in your browser so you can switch between groups. A guest name is not tied to a specific person, and anyone with the group link can pick it. After you sign in with LINE and claim it, that name's records move to your LINE account and only you can use it.
  • Content you create: group names and icons, member lists, and expense and transfer records (amount, currency, category, date, note, split method).
  • Images you upload: group covers and receipt photos. Receipts may contain store, item or other personal information, so please be careful about what you upload.

2. How we use it

This data is used only to provide the splitting features: verifying who you are, letting members of the same group see each other's names and records, and calculating settlement amounts. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell or rent it to any third party, and we do not do personalized marketing.

3. Who can see your data

  • Your display name, avatar and records can be seen only by members of the same group.
  • A group invite link contains the group identifier. Anyone who has the link and signs in can see the group name and choose to join, so share the link only with people you trust.
  • Receipt and cover images are stored behind URLs that contain an access token. Anyone who knows the full URL can open the image, so please do not share it publicly.

4. Third-party services

  • LINE (LY Corporation): provides sign-in and the LIFF environment. LINE's privacy policy applies.
  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: used for authentication, the database, file storage, the website and backend functions (hosted in Google's cloud data centers).
  • Exchange rate service open.er-api.com: when you choose a foreign currency, your device asks this service for the rate. The request contains only currency codes.

The Service does not currently use cookies for tracking, and no advertising or analytics tools are installed.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept until you delete it. In the app you can delete expenses and receipts and leave groups yourself, and a group's creator can delete the whole group (including all expenses, transfers and images).
  • If you want all your data deleted, or cannot delete it yourself, email support.acorn487@aleeas.com with your LINE display name. I will process the request within 14 days of receiving it.
  • When you leave a group or delete your own data, the shared records you already created in other people's groups are kept so that other members' calculations are not affected. Your name is turned into a "guest name" that stays in the group (your avatar is removed and it is no longer linked to your LINE account), and anyone with the group link can claim that name later. If you do not want your name kept, ask the group creator to change it before you leave, or email me at the address above. A group with only you in it, and a group with no other LINE members, is deleted entirely. Your payment methods and sign-in record (LINE user ID) are deleted as well.

6. Your rights

You can ask to view, correct or delete your personal data. Please use the contact details below.

7. Data security

Data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS, and access is protected by authentication and database security rules. No online service can be completely secure, though, so please judge how sensitive the content you upload is.

8. Children

The Service is not aimed at minors. If you are under the legal age, please use it with the consent of your legal guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date on this page will be updated. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
