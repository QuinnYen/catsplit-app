Privacy Policy

CatSplit | Last updated: November 25, 2026 | This is an English translation of the Chinese version. If the two differ, the Chinese version prevails.

CatSplit (the "Service") is a free expense-splitting tool run personally by Quinn Yen ("I" or "me"). This policy explains what data the Service collects, how it is used, and what you can do with your own data.

1. Data we collect

  • LINE account data: signing in with LINE gives us your LINE user ID, display name and avatar URL. We do not get your phone number, email address or friend list.
  • Google account data: you can sign in with Google, or link Google to an existing account in Settings. We only use the account identifier (subject), display name and avatar URL that Google provides; we do not store your email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Sign-in method mapping: so that one person can sign in in different ways, we record which sign-in method (LINE or Google) and which provider account identifier belong to your account in the Service. You can add or remove links in Settings (at least one must remain).
  • Account merge backups: when the Google or LINE account you link already has data of its own, you can choose to merge them. Before merging we back up the data of the account being merged in, and delete the backup automatically after 30 days. It is used only so the operator can help restore it if a merge goes wrong.
  • One-time link codes: when you link Google from inside LINE, we create a code that is valid for 5 minutes and can be used once, so you can finish linking in your phone\'s browser. Only a hash of the code is stored, and it is removed once used or expired.
  • Guest names: you can join without signing in to LINE by entering your own name from a group link (group members can also add a name on your behalf). We only record the name you enter and generate an identifier that stands for that name. We get no other personal data about you. The names you picked (up to 3 groups) are stored only in your browser so you can switch between groups. A guest name is not tied to a specific person, and anyone with the group link can pick it. After you sign in with LINE and claim it, that name's records move to your LINE account and only you can use it.
  • Content you create: group names and icons, member lists, and expense and transfer records (amount, currency, category, date, note, split method).
  • Payment methods: payment details you enter yourself in the app (for example a bank account or phone number), which you can choose to make public or not.
  • Personal ledgers: the ledgers and accounts you create (name, currency, balance) and your income, expense and transfer records (amount, currency, exchange rate, category, date, note). If you link a split-bill group to a ledger, a copy of your share of that group's entries (amount, title, category, date) is stored in your ledger and deleted when you unlink. Your category, budget and recurring-item settings are stored with the ledger. You can also download a backup file (JSON) of a ledger and keep it yourself; the backup file is held by you and is not uploaded to our servers, except that when downloaded inside LINE it is stored temporarily and deleted automatically after 1 day.
  • Images you upload: group covers and receipt photos. Receipts may contain store, item or other personal information, so please be careful about what you upload.

2. How we use it

This data is used only to provide the splitting features: verifying who you are, letting members of the same group see each other's names and records, and calculating settlement amounts. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell or rent it to any third party, and we do not do personalized marketing.

3. Who can see your data

  • Your display name, avatar and records can be seen only by members of the same group.
  • A group invite link contains the group identifier. Anyone who has the link and signs in can see the group name and the members' names and avatars and choose to join, so share the link only with people you trust.
  • A payment method you set to "public" can be seen by anyone signed in to the Service. One that is not public can be seen only by you.
  • Your personal ledgers (accounts and entries) can be seen only by you and are not shown to group members.
  • Operator access: the Service's data is stored in Google Firebase in the ordinary way, without end-to-end encryption, so I (the operator) technically have permission to read all of it. I access it only for maintenance, debugging, and handling your deletion or access requests, and I do not look at or use your data for any other purpose.
  • Receipt and cover images are stored behind URLs that contain an access token. Anyone who knows the full URL can open the image, so please do not share it publicly.

4. Third-party services

  • LINE (LY Corporation): provides sign-in and the LIFF environment. LINE's privacy policy applies.
  • Google sign-in (Google Identity Services): provides Google sign-in. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: used for authentication, the database, file storage, the website and backend functions (hosted in Google's cloud data centers).
  • Exchange rate service open.er-api.com: when you choose a foreign currency, your device asks this service for the rate. The request contains only currency codes.
  • Holiday data (jsDelivr, GitHub): when you use the personal ledger, your device fetches public Taiwan public-holiday data (the open-source dataset ruyut/TaiwanCalendar) from jsDelivr or GitHub. The request contains only the year and none of your data; these services see your IP address under their own privacy policies.

The Service does not currently use cookies for tracking, and no advertising or analytics tools are installed.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept until you delete it. In the app you can delete expenses and receipts and leave groups yourself, and a group's creator can delete the whole group (including all expenses, transfers and images).
  • If you want all your data deleted, or cannot delete it yourself, email support.acorn487@aleeas.com with your LINE or Google display name. I will process the request within 14 days of receiving it.
  • When you leave a group or delete your own data, the shared records you already created in other people's groups are kept so that other members' calculations are not affected. Your name is turned into a "guest name" that stays in the group (your avatar is removed and it is no longer linked to your LINE account), and anyone with the group link can claim that name later. If you do not want your name kept, ask the group creator to change it before you leave, or email me at the address above. A group with only you in it, and a group with no other LINE members, is deleted entirely. Your payment methods, personal ledgers and sign-in records (LINE user ID, Google account identifier and the sign-in method mapping) are deleted as well; any merge backup still within its retention period is removed within 30 days of the merge.

6. Your rights

You can ask to view, correct or delete your personal data. Please use the contact details below.

7. Data security

Data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS and encrypted at rest by Google, and access is protected by authentication and database security rules. No online service can be completely secure, though, so please judge how sensitive the content you upload is.

8. Children

The Service is not aimed at minors. If you are under the legal age, please use it with the consent of your legal guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date on this page will be updated. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
