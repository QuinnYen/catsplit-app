What it is

CatSplit is a free tool for splitting shared expenses among friends, for trips, dinners, shared housing and more. Everyone records spending in the same group, the app works out how much each person owes, and it suggests a settle-up plan with the fewest transfers.

The Service only records and calculates. It does not handle any money; people transfer money to each other themselves.

Features

Groups and members

  • Create groups with an icon, color and cover image, and invite friends with a link.
  • Friends who don't want to sign in can join with a guest name and still take part.

Recording expenses

  • Multi-currency expenses in 29 currencies with automatic exchange rates.
  • Several people can pay one expense; split equally, among a subset, by shares, by percentage, or by custom amounts.
  • Categories, notes, dates and receipt photos; a group budget with progress alerts.
  • Statistics and CSV export.

Settling up

  • Works out who should pay whom with the fewest transfers, with a per-person breakdown.
  • Record transfers, and set up payment details so friends know where to send money.

Personal

  • Personal savings goals: record deposits and withdrawals and track progress.
  • Traditional Chinese and English interface, with dark mode.

How to start

  1. Sign in with LINE or Google (or join a friend's group with a guest name first).
  2. Create a group, or join one with a link a friend shared.
  3. Record each expense, then open the settle-up page to see who should pay whom.

Sign-in and your data

  • LINE sign-in: we get your LINE display name and avatar. We do not get your phone number, email address or friend list.
  • Google sign-in: we only use the account identifier, display name and avatar that Google provides, to recognize you. We do not store your email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your expense data is visible only to members of the same group. It is not used for advertising or sold to third parties.
  • You can delete your own data in the app at any time. See the Privacy Policy for details.
